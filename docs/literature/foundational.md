# Foundational PINN Papers (1998–2020)

These papers established the core PINN methodology and early applications.

## The Canon

### Raissi, Perdikaris, Karniadakis (2019)
**Physics-informed neural networks: A deep learning framework for solving forward and inverse problems involving nonlinear partial differential equations**

Citation count: **16,000+**  
[DOI:10.1016/j.jcp.2018.10.045](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2018.10.045)

The canonical paper. Everyone cites this. Start here.

### Raissi, Perdikaris, Karniadakis (2017 Part I & II)
**Physics Informed Deep Learning (Part I & II)**

- Part I: Data-driven Solutions of Nonlinear PDEs [arXiv:1711.10561](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10561)
- Part II: Data-driven Discovery of PDEs [arXiv:1711.10566](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.10566)

Original preprints introducing PINNs and coefficient discovery.

## Early Precursors

### Lagaris, Likas, Fotiadis (1998)
**Artificial neural networks for solving ordinary and partial differential equations**

Citation count: ~5,000  
[DOI:10.1109/72.712178](https://doi.org/10.1109/72.712178)

Historical foundation: first systematic NN approach to DEs.

### Han, Jentzen, E (2018)
**Solving high-dimensional partial differential equations using deep learning**

[DOI:10.1073/pnas.1718942115](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1718942115)

Showed deep learning solves 100+ dimensional PDEs via control reformulation.

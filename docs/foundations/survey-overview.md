# Major PINN Surveys and Review Papers

This section summarizes the most important survey and review papers covering PINNs and physics-informed machine learning.

## Authoritative Reviews

### Karniadakis et al. (2021) - Nature Reviews Physics

**Title**: Physics-informed machine learning  
**Citation**: ~5,000 citations  
**Link**: [Nature Reviews Physics](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-021-00314-5)

Comprehensive overview of:
- PINNs, operator learning, physics-inspired architectures
- Applications across domains
- Theoretical perspectives and open challenges

### Cuomo et al. (2022) - PINN Survey

**Title**: Scientific Machine Learning through Physics-Informed Neural Networks  
**Citation**: ~2,500 citations  
**Link**: [arXiv:2201.05624](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.05624)

Most-cited PINN-specific survey covering:
- Building blocks and variants (VPINN, CPINN, XPINN)
- Learning theory
- Toolsets and implementations
- Future directions

### 2025 Comprehensive Review

**Title**: Physics-informed neural networks for PDE Problems: A comprehensive review  
**Link**: [DOI:10.1007/s10462-025-11322-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11322-7)

Recent overview of:
- Modern architectures
- Data resampling techniques
- Loss function innovations
- Feature embeddings
- 2025 perspective on future directions

### From PINNs to PIKANs (2024)

**Title**: From PINNs to PIKANs: Recent Advances in Physics-Informed Machine Learning  
**Authors**: Toscano, Oommen, Varghese, Zou, Ahmadi Daryakenari, Wu, Karniadakis  
**Link**: [arXiv:2410.13228](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13228)

Evolution from PINNs through neural operators to Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks.
